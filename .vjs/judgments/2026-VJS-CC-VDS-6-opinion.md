# [2026] VJS-CC-VIBE-DESIGN-SYSTEM 6 - The prune power

**County Court, Marbury J.** SUBMISSION-2026-07-31-191142 (SUBMISSION-VDS-007).

A delete power that exists in code and nowhere in law is cured by legislating it, not by
withdrawing it (an unreadable record store fails its readers on schedule, and the next
ungoverned cleanup script is this question again without a court) and not by scratch
(a record of deletions that can itself be deleted is not a record). VDS.md S-4(5) now
carries the keep-rules on its face and records git as the append-only store behind the
working set, which is what makes pruning housekeeping rather than destruction. Prune
never runs unattended: deletion is an act a person initiates with the rules in front of
them, and the enforced interim test becomes the permanent gate.
