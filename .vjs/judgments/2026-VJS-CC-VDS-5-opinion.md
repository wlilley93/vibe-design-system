# [2026] VJS-CC-VIBE-DESIGN-SYSTEM 5 - Three questions on the proof statutes

**County Court, Halden J.** Consolidated (SUBMISSION-VDS-004..006). Filed as
SUBMISSION-2026-07-31-191011.

Forced drain: the absolute drain stays the default and the only executive path. A
retirement may complete over live demand only under an order of this court naming that
demand route by route on its face - the party wanting the retirement declaring the
breakage acceptable is the self-certification this series has refused three times today.

The primitive floor: an enumerated governed set of elements sits below the composition
floor. An unstated floor lets hand-rolled divs styled as buttons compose invisibly;
failing every bare element is the cry-wolf failure at maximum volume. A list a reader can
disagree with, changed by ordinary governed edits, is the form a floor should take.

The pin row: name plus agreement bit, and not as a discretion - S-2(8) already treats a
recoverable encoding as a realisation, and a per-value digest over an invertible domain
is recoverable by dictionary (measured: 52 values recovered from 26 rows in 27 seconds).
The pin type already conforms; this holding ratifies it and forbids regression. Salting
is refused: the salt is a key someone holds while the record pretends nobody can invert.
