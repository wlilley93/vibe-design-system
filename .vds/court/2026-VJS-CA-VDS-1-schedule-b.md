# [2026] VJS-CA-VDS 1 - SCHEDULE B, as implemented

**What this file is.** Order 1 of the judgment says that an amendment proposed in the
opinions is carried only where two or more judges require the same substantive correction on
the same ground; every other proposal "is recorded in Schedule B, binds nothing, and is
available to a future submission". This is the implementing repository's record of those
eleven items, and of what was done about each.

It exists because a not-carried amendment is the easiest thing in a governance corpus to
lose. It is not law, so nothing enforces it; it is not refused, so nothing files it; and
next time the same ground comes up the finding has to be made again from scratch, by
somebody who does not know it was already made. The judgment itself says why the record is
kept: "Recorded so the 'narrowest amendment' computation is auditable."

**The rule this repository follows for a Schedule B item.** It binds nothing, so it is not
written into the statute as law and no proof cites it as authority. Where the item names a
defect this repository agrees is real, the engine change may be BUILT, marked as drafted on
its face, and put to the bench on its own case file - which is this lane's standing posture
and is what happened to item 1. Where it is not built, it is recorded here and left
available, and this file is the thing a future drafter reads first.

## The two items a reader should carry away

**Item 8 is the largest live gap.** `visual_review` and the geometry authority limb are
authority-aware and `screen_parity` is not, so on the day the register's first row lands
those three kinds will disagree about what an unregistered surface is: two will report
coverage owed and one will score a deviation. The judgment's own reasoning against two
regimes ("one rule and a disagreement") applies to that split with full force, and it was
left uncarried only because one judge raised it.

**Item 6 is the one that could switch a clause off silently.** The measurement-coverage
rules live inside a proof that refuses to run at all on a stale screens ledger or a
duplicated code coordinate. Both have fired on the subscriber estate. If they fire again,
S-5(9) reports nothing and the run says PRECONDITION FAILED for a reason that has nothing to
do with measurement coverage - which is precisely the shape this whole lane exists to make
visible, arriving inside the cure.
