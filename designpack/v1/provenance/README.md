# Provenance — designpack v1

- **What:** the VDS specification (VDS.md, S-1..S-15) vendored verbatim as the
  pack's doctrine, digest recorded in `manifest.toml`.
- **Commencement:** NOT COMMENCED (VDS S-15(1)). No assent event exists; this
  pack carries doctrine to subscribers pinned and read-only, and confers no
  warrant authority of its own.
- **Reserved clauses:** every clause VDS.md marks RESERVED is answered in the
  jurisdiction that ships VDS.md (this repository). Subscribers receive the
  answers through this pack and do not re-file them (doctor D10, Upstream).
- **Judgments:** adjudicated matters (e.g. [2026] VJS-CA-VDS 1) live in the
  VJS lawpack that governs this repository; they bind through VJS, and are not
  duplicated here — a second copy of a ruling is a second authority.
